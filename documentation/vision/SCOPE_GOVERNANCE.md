# SahelFlow 1.0 — Scope Governance

> **Status:** Active scope-control authority  
> **Founder scope authority:** `documentation/product/LAUNCH_SCOPE_AND_ENTITLEMENTS.md`

The vision recovery intentionally captures more detail than the frozen launch table. That richness must not silently expand SahelFlow 1.0.

## Scope classes

Every capability, page function or interaction must be classified before implementation.

### L1 — Founder-approved launch obligation

Explicitly required by the Constitution, Founder Decisions or Frozen Launch Scope. It must be complete for Stable unless a new founder decision changes it.

Examples: professional teams, products/variants/stock, customer risk, order intake, WhatsApp, AI privacy/fallback, certified couriers, hybrid commerce sync, returns/refunds, COD, accounting, analytics, automations, hosted storefronts, operational PWA, zero-knowledge recovery, founder administration and signed updates.

### L2 — Necessary launch-depth requirement

Not always named word-for-word in the launch table, but necessary to make an L1 outcome complete, accessible, safe, understandable or operable.

Examples: loading/error/degraded states, audit timeline visibility, import preview, provider health, entity connections, keyboard access, Arabic bidi handling, retry queues, conflict UI, migration maintenance and recovery guidance.

L2 requirements must cite the L1 outcome, invariant or evidence gate they enable.

### L3 — Recovered product-depth candidate

A durable idea from earlier plans or research that could materially improve the product but is not automatically required for Stable.

Examples may include barcode workflows, recurring expenses, broad WhatsApp broadcasting, advanced drag-and-drop customization, extensive marketing/blog breadth, additional report formats, recent-item systems or optional productivity enhancements.

L3 work requires explicit prioritization. It may be promoted to L1/L2 by a founder decision or by proving it is necessary to satisfy an existing launch outcome. Otherwise it belongs after the launch-critical dependency path.

### L4 — Post-1.0 or excluded

Ideas outside the frozen launch scope, conditional capabilities not yet certified, and explicit exclusions.

Examples: native Android, macOS/Linux Stable, marketplace/multi-country, payroll/attendance, Meta/TikTok inbox, enterprise SSO, arbitrary storefront code, automated BaridiMob approval and product-funded Gemini.

## Interpretation rule for the capability atlas

`FUNCTIONAL_CAPABILITY_ATLAS.md` is a complete recovery map, not permission to implement every bullet immediately.

- Items directly supported by the Frozen Launch Scope are L1.
- Behaviors necessary for safety, completeness, accessibility, RTL, low-end parity or recovery are L2.
- Historical richness not clearly required is L3 until classified.
- Conditional/excluded items remain L4 or hidden pending certification.

No issue may cite the atlas alone. It must include a scope class and the controlling founder/launch clause.

## Scope-change gate

A scope change must state:

- current class;
- requested class;
- controlling founder decision or proposed new decision;
- user outcome;
- dependency and migration effect;
- economic, security, legal and support impact;
- evidence required;
- what is displaced if capacity is fixed.

Creativity is encouraged inside the approved outcome. Untracked expansion is not.
