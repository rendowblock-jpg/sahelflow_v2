- macOS/Linux Stable releases;
- marketplace or multi-country expansion;
- payroll, attendance or surveillance;
- Meta/TikTok inbox;
- arbitrary storefront JavaScript;

## 5. Historical research freshness

Time-sensitive historical research—including provider APIs, Cloudflare prices/limits, Gemini model availability, competitor capabilities and legal interpretation—must be revalidated before implementation or public claims.

Historical factual claims are useful leads, not current truth.
