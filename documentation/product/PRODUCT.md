# SahelFlow 1.0 — Product Contract

> **Status:** Founder-approved product baseline
> **Last consolidated:** 2026-07-24
> **Public release:** SahelFlow 1.0 / app version 1.0.0
> **Commercial edition:** One complete edition

## 1. Product identity

SahelFlow is the complete Windows-first operations system for Algerian
cash-on-delivery sellers. Public copy may describe it as an all-in-one COD
operations app; it must not imply generic ERP scope or claim capabilities that
have not passed their release evidence.

It is:

- Algeria-first;
- COD-first;
- WhatsApp-first;
- local-first, with an explicitly bounded cloud control plane;
- Arabic, French, and English;
- optimized for low-end Windows hardware and constrained networks;
- rigorous about money, stock, customer data, authorization, recovery, and auditability.

SahelFlow is not a generic CRM, a website-builder template pack, a cloud database with a desktop shell, or an MVP released under premium marketing language.

### Scope classes

- **Required** — part of the Founder-approved SahelFlow 1.0 Stable promise.
- **Conditional** — named capability that remains hidden, experimental or
  narrowly described until certification.
- **Depth requirement** — behavior and states required to make a Required
  capability professionally complete.
- **Candidate** — useful idea or recovered capability that is not yet a launch
  promise.
- **Excluded** — explicitly outside SahelFlow 1.0.

Ambiguous scope defaults to Candidate.

## 2. Product authority model

- One Windows installation is the authoritative business system for a standard license.
- Each shop has an isolated authoritative local database.
- The authoritative desktop owns canonical orders, customers, inventory, money, refunds, accounting, delivery state, team authorization, audit history, and automation execution.
- Cloudflare provides the approved control plane, relay, storefront runtime, durable ingress, notifications, licensing support, encrypted backup storage, and remote coordination.
- Android/browser clients receive minimized, permission-filtered projections and submit signed commands.
- No cloud projection, queue, cache, webhook, or mobile UI may claim a canonical business mutation succeeded before the desktop transaction commits.
- Multiple competing authoritative Windows databases and silent multi-master merge are prohibited.

## 3. Commercial promise

SahelFlow 1.0 costs **35,000 DZD one-time** for one complete edition.

There is:

- no subscription;
- no feature tier;
- no team plan;
- no per-seat fee;
- no recurring per-shop fee;
- no required recurring SahelFlow cloud payment from the seller.

The purchase grants perpetual local use of major release 1. Same-major security, compatibility, bug-fix, and quality updates are included during the guaranteed support policy. Future major releases may be optional paid upgrades and may never be forced silently.

## 4. Support and continuity promise

Major release 1 receives at least **five years** of guaranteed same-major maintenance and SahelFlow-controlled connected-service continuity from its official public stable launch date.

After the guarantee:

- the permanent local license does not expire;
- local data remains accessible and exportable;
- SahelFlow must not disable the purchased release merely to force an upgrade;
- connected services may continue where technically and economically sustainable;
- planned material discontinuation normally requires at least 12 months' notice and applicable export/migration tooling.

Third-party providers remain outside SahelFlow's absolute control, but every certified integration requires monitoring, dated contract evidence, visible degraded states, and the safest feasible fallback.

## 5. Launch edition boundaries

The base edition includes:

- 1 primary owner;
- 10 active team members;
- 2 registered personal devices per member;
- 3 registered owner remote devices;
- 5 active shops;
- 1 hosted storefront per entitled shop;
- 1 default SahelFlow subdomain per storefront;
- 1 certified custom subdomain per storefront after domain certification;
- 20 GB shared encrypted cloud-backup storage;
- 2 GB storefront media per included storefront, with a 10 GB shared base allowance.

Up to five extra shops may be purchased at **5,000 DZD one-time per shop**, producing an initial certified maximum of 10 shops. Each expansion includes a storefront, subdomain entitlements, 2 GB media, 4 GB backup capacity, and the complete feature set for that shop.

Limits are published security, abuse-control, performance, and economic boundaries—not hidden product tiers.

## 6. Professional teams

SahelFlow 1.0 includes real multi-user operations under the authoritative owner installation:

- individual cryptographic identities;
- per-shop membership;
- safe role presets and custom permissions;
- field-level and action-level authorization;
- workgroups, assignments, queues, internal comments, mentions, and handovers;
- optional high-risk two-person approvals;
- immediate device/session/member revocation;
- trusted actor attribution and complete audit trails;
- local operator profiles and secure remote browser/PWA access.

Shared staff accounts, client-only authorization, silent last-write-wins, employee surveillance, payroll, attendance, Active Directory, enterprise SSO, and independent team databases are excluded from the first release.

## 7. Android and browser companion

The installable Android PWA and responsive browser workspace are full operational companions with limited administration.

They support approved daily work such as dashboards, orders, confirmation queues, AI-draft review, customer contact handoff, notes, customer risk/history, products/stock lookup, delivery/return visibility, inbox preview, limited analytics, team queues, and durable command status.

Destructive administration, license transfer, recovery keys, backup restoration, integration secrets, shop deletion, advanced diagnostics, and other high-risk administration remain desktop-only unless separately certified.

## 8. Storefront standard

SahelFlow operates one shared multi-tenant storefront platform with isolated tenant data and immutable releases.

Every entitled shop receives:

- a default `seller-slug.sahelflow.app` store;
- controlled brand customization;
- private preview;
- validation;
- atomic publish;
- version history and rollback;
- durable COD checkout while the desktop is offline;
- bounded cloud stock allocation to prevent overselling;
- certified custom-subdomain support.

Launch includes three genuinely distinct templates:

1. Minimal Conversion;
2. Visual Editorial;
3. Bold COD Landing.

The north-star outcome is **profitable confirmed and delivered COD orders per 1,000 qualified visitors**. Public `best-in-market` claims require comparative evidence, real seller results, production performance data, and independent review.

## 9. Backup and recovery

Cloud backup is mandatory for the public launch and is zero-knowledge by design:

- encryption occurs on the seller's Windows device before upload;
- each license has a Backup Root Key;
- each backup has a unique data-encryption key;
- the seller receives an independent recovery kit;
- optional assisted recovery requires both a protected enrolled-device share and a separate founder offline share;
- SahelFlow or Cloudflare alone cannot decrypt seller backups;
- backups are integrity-checked, restore-tested, versioned, and protected against silent overwrite/deletion;
- a failed restore leaves the existing installation unchanged.

Normal retention is 7 daily, 4 weekly, 6 monthly, and up to 3 pinned points per shop, bounded by the published storage allowance.

## 10. Licensing and payment

- Trial duration is 7 days.
- Initial trial issuance requires the online licensing service and is limited to one trial per recognized machine.
- Trial licenses are signed by a dedicated trial-only key; the permanent private signing key remains offline.
- Trial expiry produces complete product lockout with only the approved licensing, payment, extension, support, and minimal diagnostic shell.
- Trial expiry preserves data but does not allow viewing, exporting, operating, or bypassing through mobile/cache/API routes.
- Permanent-license payment uses a professional manual BaridiMob/CCP verification workflow.
- Screenshots and customer references are supporting evidence only; the founder verifies the actual receiving account.
- Permanent license and extra-shop entitlements are signed offline.
- Legitimate machine replacement is included without an activation fee.
- One authoritative Windows installation is active at a time.

## 11. AI policy

SahelFlow uses the seller's own Google AI Studio Gemini API key. The launch default is the currently certified stable model `gemini-3.5-flash`, subject to versioned provider-policy updates.

- SahelFlow does not fund normal seller inference or pool seller requests through a SahelFlow Gemini key.
- Core product operation never depends on AI availability.
- The default free-tier mode is privacy-safe and must not silently transmit raw customer PII, confidential records, credentials, financial details, or complete WhatsApp histories.
- Local deterministic extraction, tokenization, redaction, structured validation, and manual fallback are required.
- A professional Arabic/French/English wizard guides key creation, restrictions, secure storage, safe testing, privacy acknowledgement, quota/error diagnosis, rotation, and disconnection.
- AI suggestions are drafts or explicitly approved actions, never unreviewed owner-authority mutations.

## 12. Synchronization and external events

Commerce integrations use platform-specific hybrid synchronization:

- certified webhooks/REST hooks provide low-latency notification;
- scheduled reconciliation remains mandatory;
- events are authenticated, deduplicated, durably persisted before acknowledgement, encrypted before long-term storage, retried idempotently, and repaired through overlap scans;
- checkpoints never advance past an untracked failure;
- Shopify and WooCommerce use full hybrid synchronization after live certification;
- YouCan uses conservative hooks plus polling and scheduled wider reconciliation until update-event behavior is proven.

Webhooks are never the sole source of truth.

## 13. Low-end-first performance

SahelFlow must be engineered for functional parity on low-end Windows systems rather than requiring strong hardware to hide inefficient architecture.

Primary optimization floor:

- 64-bit x86 Windows;
- dual-core class CPU;
- 4 GB RAM;
- HDD or SSD;
- 1366×768 display;
- required runtime capabilities available.

Windows 10 22H2, unsupported-CPU Windows 11, Tiny11/modified builds, HDD systems, and virtual machines are targeted for capability-based functional compatibility where required components exist. SahelFlow must identify missing components precisely rather than issuing vague hardware rejection.

The founder's ThinkPad T470 is a mandatory daily-reference machine. A materially weaker 4 GB dual-core device is the floor-reference machine.

Architecture choices must be changed when they cannot meet the performance envelope. The hardware floor may not be raised merely to preserve a convenient framework or process topology.

## 14. Security and privacy

Launch requires:

- OS-protected secret and license storage where available;
- explicit threat models and residual-risk disclosure;
- application-layer encryption for private cloud payloads;
- strict tenant, shop, member, device, and license boundaries;
- trusted actor context derived from authenticated sessions;
- transactional audit and outbox behavior for critical mutations;
- no plaintext secrets in browser storage, source code, logs, R2, D1, or Queue payloads;
- replay protection, idempotency, rate limiting, session revocation, conflict handling, and recovery paths;
- Law 18-07 review for every applicable data class.

Security language must describe verified behavior, not intended design.

## 15. Launch gate

SahelFlow 1.0 cannot become Stable until all launch-required systems satisfy their engineering acceptance criteria and evidence gates, including:

- frozen scope and entitlement policy;
- zero unresolved P0/P1 defects;
- packaged Windows end-to-end testing;
- security and privacy review;
- backup/restore and migration drills;
- real provider certification;
- low-end reference-device performance;
- tenant/team/authorization isolation tests;
- accessibility and Arabic RTL validation;
- storefront durability and checkout integrity;
- controlled beta with representative Algerian COD sellers;
- evidence-backed public claims.

## 16. Explicit launch exclusions

Unless separately amended and certified, the first release excludes:

- native Android application;
- macOS and native Linux releases;
- ARM64 and Wine equivalence claims;
- multiple authoritative Windows databases;
- cloud multi-master business database;
- unlimited shops, members, storage, or traffic;
- enterprise SSO and Active Directory;
- TikTok/Meta inbox integrations;
- marketplace, multi-country, payroll, attendance, employee surveillance, and arbitrary storefront JavaScript/HTML;
- automated BaridiMob/CCP account monitoring or payment approval.

## 17. Public truth rule

Every public statement must match verified behavior at a named release and commit. Historical plans, internal versions, prototypes, placeholders, adapter existence, or test counts cannot be converted into product claims without current evidence.

## 20. Cloud fair-use validation envelope

Before connected entitlements become final or public, the shared architecture
and continuity reserve must be validated at 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 seller
workspaces. At minimum, tests model the existing per-permanent-license monthly
envelope:

- 250,000 remote commands;
- 100,000 operational notifications;
- 250,000 storefront sessions across all stores;
- 25,000 durable COD submissions;
- associated provider, reconciliation, release, domain, media and backup work.

Crossing a published boundary triggers capacity/abuse review, not silent local
lockout, destructive retention changes or a hidden recurring charge.

## 21. Measurable launch targets

### Low-end desktop

On the 4 GB dual-core floor device with representative data:

- cold usable shell no slower than 15 seconds p95 on entry SSD and 25 seconds
  on HDD;
- ordinary navigation acknowledges input within 100 ms and becomes usable
  within 1.5 seconds p95;
- indexed order/customer search within 750 ms p95;
- ordinary local mutation within 1 second p95, excluding provider latency;
- no ordinary interaction freeze longer than 200 ms;
- steady working-set target no greater than 750 MB with WhatsApp connected and
  no heavy job;
- no sustained memory growth over an eight-hour session.

On the Founder ThinkPad T470 class:

- cold launch within 8 seconds p95;
- ordinary navigation within 700 ms p95;
- indexed search within 350 ms p95;
- ordinary local mutation within 500 ms p95.

The accepted Internal.5 reopen is functionally proven but slower than desired;
it creates performance work and does not waive these Stable targets.

### Hosted storefront

- mobile LCP p75 no greater than 1.8 seconds;
- mobile INP p75 no greater than 150 ms;
- CLS p75 no greater than 0.05;
- checkout API p95 no greater than 500 ms under approved regional tests;
- availability objective at least 99.95%;
- accepted checkout without durable receipt: zero;
- duplicate canonical effect under retry: zero;
- cross-tenant leakage: zero;
- displayed/canonical price mismatch: zero;
- WCAG 2.2 AA on launch-critical journeys.

These are target evidence requirements, never claims about the current
implementation.
