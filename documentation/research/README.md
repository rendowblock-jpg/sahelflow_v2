# SahelFlow research archive

> **Status:** Reference material, not active product or engineering authority

This directory preserves audits, comparative UX studies, provider investigation and historical analysis that may inform implementation. Individual files may contain language such as “the spec,” “must,” “launch,” “current,” or “gold standard” from the time they were written. That language does not override the active authority chain.

## How to use research

1. Start with root `AGENTS.md` and the active wave.
2. Read the controlling product, experience and engineering authorities first.
3. Use a research document only for the exact question it investigated.
4. Revalidate time-sensitive provider, platform, library, legal, market and performance claims.
5. Verify historical source-code findings against the current executable baseline.
6. Adopt a durable conclusion by updating the active document that owns it; do not treat the research file itself as the final decision.

## Precedence

- Founder/product authority controls scope and promises.
- Experience authority controls included capability/journey/frontend quality.
- Engineering authority controls target invariants and accepted decisions.
- Current-to-Target Analysis controls current source reality.
- Provider registry controls public integration claims.
- Research supplies evidence, alternatives and challenge only.
