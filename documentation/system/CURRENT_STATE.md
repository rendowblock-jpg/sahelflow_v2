## Executive truth

SahelFlow is a Windows-first local-first application for Algerian COD operations
with substantial protected business, identity, licensing, provider, recovery and
desktop authority. The backend/engine is the asset to preserve. It is not yet a
commercially certified Stable release and the current installed frontend is not a
Founder-accepted AAA experience.

Internal.14 is public and was installed through the normal updater on the Founder
T470. The exact installed executable reports `1.0.0-internal.14`; a matching
Founder-offline permanent entitlement activated and survived close/reopen, and
the existing dashboard/workspace became usable after reopen. Internal.14 is not
Founder-accepted.

The installed session exposed engineering and product-experience failures that
must be resolved before Phase 8 increases system complexity:

- the sole production trial-service route was unreachable from the Founder
  Algerian network; issue #230 retains resilient trial activation;
- the first render after successful permanent activation became blank until
  close/reopen;
- ordinary startup was observed to take many minutes and remains to be measured
  under issue #226;
- historical PR #200/#207 CI exception mechanisms remain active and can suppress
  evidence lanes that future protected changes may require;
- Phase 4 replacement-install post-restore page-level protected-data evidence
  remains retained in issue #214;
- active documentation and source comments contain bounded stale authority/debt
  that must be reconciled without reopening already-proven canonical engines;
- the Founder-installed frontend was rejected as a whole-product experience,
  including typography/density, locale/RTL transitions, themes, motion,
  navigation, warnings, charts and the Inbox, AI Agents and Settings workspaces.

The Phase 5/6 protected source/browser checkpoints remain valid historical
evidence for what those exact heads proved. They do **not** override the later
installed Founder rejection and they do not certify the current frontend as AAA.

## Founder-installed frontend assessment

The Founder does not need to enumerate every margin, label or page defect. The
screenshots and installed use establish a systemic frontend-quality problem, so
the repair model is root-cause-first rather than screenshot-by-screenshot.

The mandatory frontend outcomes before Phase 8 are:

- a deliberate Arabic/Latin typography system with professional Arabic joining,
  readable hierarchy and operationally comfortable sizing;
- readable density at the T470/1366×768 reference instead of shrinking text or
  controls to manufacture space;
- atomic AR/FR/EN locale and LTR/RTL switching with no multi-second stale text,
  stale geometry, wrong-side sidebar or restart requirement;
- a semantic token-based theme system with excellent light and dark foundations
  plus coordinated accent/color families rather than page-local color overrides;
- a restrained motion system for navigation, overlays, state changes, loading,
  lists, theme/locale changes and charts, with reduced-motion and low-resource
  behavior preserved;
- logical flow-relative RTL geometry across shell, navigation, forms, tables,
  charts, icons and mixed Arabic/Latin/number content;
- a shallow, task-shaped sidebar where primary destinations are visible and only
  genuine child destinations are nested;
- a warning hierarchy where routine configuration/data-quality notices are
  compact and contextual, while dominant/blocking treatment is reserved for
  genuinely blocking consequences;
- a richer analytical visualization grammar chosen by seller decision/task,
  including useful comparison, context, inspection and drill-down where the data
  supports it;
- product-level redesign of Inbox, AI Agents and Settings based on their actual
  backend capabilities and seller workflows, not cosmetic restyling;
- a route-wide self-audit of the remaining dashboard, order, customer, product,
  delivery, returns, COD/accounting, analytics, automation, setup, licensing and
  administration surfaces so the Founder is not used as a pixel-by-pixel QA
  substitute.

Supporting research and source references for these requirements are retained in
`documentation/archive/research/PRE_PHASE8_FRONTEND_STABILIZATION_RESEARCH-2026-08-10.md`.
Research is input to the active authorities, not a second roadmap.

finding, compatibility requirement or necessary contract change with its normal
evidence.

canonical business transitions and recovery states rather than inventing parallel
client truth.
