# ChatGPT logo files

`chatgpt-logo-black.svg` and `chatgpt-logo-white.svg` are the two logo files that OpenAI's own
"Sign in with ChatGPT" documentation loads for its approved button formats (a black button with the white
logo, or a white button with the black logo). They are kept byte-identical to OpenAI's files.

Source:
- https://developers.openai.com/assets/siwc/sign-in-buttons/chatgpt-logo-black.svg
- https://developers.openai.com/assets/siwc/sign-in-buttons/chatgpt-logo-white.svg

ChatGPT and the ChatGPT logo are trademarks of OpenAI; used unmodified on the Continue with ChatGPT button
as in OpenAI's Sign in with ChatGPT guidelines; SahelFlow is not endorsed by OpenAI.

Use them only on that button (src/components/settings/chatgpt-plan-card.tsx). Do not recolor, crop, animate or restyle them.
