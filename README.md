wave: professional operational density, complete states, real authority and data,
route-by-route visual regression and Founder visual acceptance.

## Binding product shape

- Windows x64 desktop is canonical for operational business mutations.
- One complete edition costs 35,000 DZD one-time.
- Five included shops and up to five paid expansions.
- One owner plus ten active team members under approved device limits.
- Arabic, French and English require equivalent product depth and real RTL/LTR
  behavior.
- Functional floor: dual-core x64 Windows, 4 GB RAM, HDD or SSD, 1366×768.
- ThinkPad T470 is mandatory daily reference hardware.
- Shared cloud, PWA, hosted storefront and zero-knowledge backup remain bounded by
  authority, security, recovery and economics gates.
- Seller-owned Gemini key supports optional privacy-controlled AI.
